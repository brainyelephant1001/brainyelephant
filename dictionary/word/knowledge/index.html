Wiktionary meanings

View source ↗
  1. Noununcountableusually

    The fact of knowing about something; general understanding or familiarity with a subject, place, situation etc.

  2. Noununcountableusually

    Awareness of a particular fact or situation; a state of having been informed or made aware of something.

  3. Noununcountableusually

    Intellectual understanding; the state of appreciating truth or information.

  4. Noununcountableusually

    Familiarity or understanding of a particular skill, branch of learning etc.

  5. Noununcountableusually

    Justified true belief

  6. Nounarchaicuncountableusually

    Sexual intimacy or intercourse (now usually in phrase carnal knowledge).

  7. Nounobsoleteuncountableusually

    Information or intelligence about something; notice.

  8. Noununcountableusually

    The total of what is known; all information and products of learning.

  9. Nouncountableusually

    Something that can be known; a branch of learning; a piece of information; a science.

  10. Nounobsoleteuncountableusually

    Acknowledgement.

  11. Nounobsoleteuncountableusually

    Notice, awareness.

  12. NounUKinformaluncountableusually

    The deep familiarity with certain routes and places of interest required by taxicab drivers working in London, England.

  13. Verbobsolete

    To confess as true; to acknowledge.

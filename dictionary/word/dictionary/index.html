Wiktionary meanings

View source ↗
  1. Noun

    A reference work listing words or names from one or more languages, usually ordered alphabetically, explaining each term's meanings or senses, often also containing information on its etymology, pronunciation, usage, semantic relations, translations, as well as other relevant information.

  2. Noun

    A reference work listing words or names from one or more languages, usually ordered alphabetically, explaining each term's meanings or senses, often also containing information on its etymology, pronunciation, usage, semantic relations, translations, as well as other relevant information.

    A synchronic dictionary of a standardised language held to only contain words that are properly part of the language.

  3. Nounbroadly

    A reference work listing words or names from one or more languages, usually ordered alphabetically, explaining each term's meanings or senses, often also containing information on its etymology, pronunciation, usage, semantic relations, translations, as well as other relevant information.

    A reference work on a particular subject or activity in which the entries are arranged alphabetically; an alphabetical encyclopedia.

  4. Nounfiguratively

    The collection of words used or understood by a particular individual.

  5. Nounfiguratively

    Someone or something regarded as a repository or compendium of information.

  6. Noun

    An associative array; a data structure where each value is referenced by a particular key, analogous to words and definitions in a dictionary (noun sense 1).

  7. Verbtransitive

    To look up in a dictionary.

  8. Verbtransitive

    To add to a dictionary.

  9. Verbintransitiverare

    To compile a dictionary.

THE MEANING OF A WORD

research.

Meanings, usage and a little more understanding.

WordNet meanings

View source ↗
  1. Noun

    systematic investigation to establish facts

  2. Noun

    a search for knowledge

    their pottery deserves more research than it has received
  3. Verb

    attempt to find out in a systematically and scientific manner

    The student researched the history of that word
  4. Verb

    inquire into

    the students had to research the history of the Second World War for their history project
    He searched for information on his relatives on the web
    Scientists are exploring the nature of consciousness

Wiktionary meanings

  1. Nouncountabledialectaluncountableusually

    Diligent inquiry or examination to seek or revise facts, principles, theories, applications, etc. (that are currently available, or that are familiar to oneself).

  2. Nouncountabledatedusually

    A particular instance or piece of research.

  3. Verbtransitive

    To search or examine with continued care; to seek diligently.

  4. Verbintransitive

    To make an extensive investigation into.

  5. Verbtransitive

    To search again.

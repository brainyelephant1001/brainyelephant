THE MEANING OF A WORD

here.

Meanings, usage and a little more understanding.

WordNet meanings

View source ↗
  1. Adjective

    being here now

    is everyone here?
  2. Noun

    the present location; this place; location, proximal pronoun; demonstrative pronoun, location; quantifier: demonstrative determiner, singular, proximal

    where do we go from here?
  3. Adverb

    in or at this place; where the speaker or writer is

    I work here
    turn here
    radio waves received here on Earth
  4. Adverb

    in this circumstance or respect or on this point or detail

    what do we have here?
    here I must disagree
  5. Adverb

    to this place (especially toward the speaker)

    come here, please
  6. Adverb

    at this time; now

    we'll adjourn here for lunch and discuss the remaining issues this afternoon

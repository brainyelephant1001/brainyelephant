THE MEANING OF A WORD

future.

Meanings, usage and a little more understanding.

WordNet meanings

View source ↗
  1. Adjective

    yet to be or coming

    some future historian will evaluate him
  2. Adjective

    effective in or looking toward the future

    he was preparing for future employment opportunities
  3. Adjective

    (of elected officers) elected but not yet serving

    our next president
  4. Adjective

    a verb tense or other formation referring to events or states that have not yet happened

    future auxiliary
  5. Noun

    the time yet to come

  6. Noun

    a verb tense that expresses actions or states in the future

  7. Noun

    bulk commodities bought or sold at an agreed price for delivery at a specified future date

What we changed

Selected and combined word spellings, preserved source meanings and usage labels, normalised parts of speech, and reformatted the data into searchable pages. Wiktionary quotations, examples, audio, images and etymology were not imported. Source examples retained from WordNet remain credited to that source. The adapted merged dictionary collection is CC BY-SA 4.0. Its separately licensed source material keeps the original grants.

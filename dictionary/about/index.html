What this test does

Search the merged dictionary and open a separate, readable page for each entry. Titles follow the “[word] meaning and definition” pattern. Pages contain real server-rendered definitions, not just a search box that search engines cannot read.

What we still need to build

Reviewed community submissions, original learning examples and a carefully selected vocabulary for children. The full source collection can contain adult or offensive language, labelled where the source provides labels; this preview offers a small selected vocabulary. A dictionary entry is not proof of game acceptance.

Testing search visibility

This is a small public preview on brainyelephant.com. Its word pages are accessible to search engines; indexing and ranking take time and are not guaranteed. A prepared sitemap contains a small starter selection of actual word pages. Neither titles nor the sitemap guarantee a place in search results.

Scientific Roots: privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Scientific Roots is provided by Philip Ray, trading as Brainy Elephant. Contact: brainyelephant1001@gmail.com.

Information in the app

Scientific Roots works offline. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases or tracking SDKs. The app does not collect, transmit or share your personal information, answers, scores or learning progress.

Local progress

Completed lessons, practice answers, unfinished assessments and results are stored locally in the app's private storage. You can remove these records using “Reset local learning data” in About, privacy and sources, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling. Android backup is disabled for this release. Copy any results summary you want to keep before deleting data. We cannot restore records we do not receive.

External links and support

Optional source and website links open in your external browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your enquiry; do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Google Play may separately process store, purchase and device information under Google's own policies.

Permissions and changes

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or access to shared files. If a future version changes its data practices, this policy and the Play listing will be updated.

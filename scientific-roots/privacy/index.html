Scientific Roots: privacy policy

Updated 7 November 2026. Scientific Roots is provided by Philip Ray, trading as Brainy Elephant. Contact: brainyelephant1001@gmail.com.

Learning records

Lessons and assessments work offline. Your completed lessons, practice answers, unfinished assessments and results are stored in the app's private local storage. These learning records are not sent to Brainy Elephant or Google AdMob. There are no accounts or in-app purchases. Use “Reset local learning data” in About to remove course records. Clearing app data or uninstalling can also remove them. Android backup is disabled. We cannot restore records we do not receive.

Banner advertising in version 1.1 and later

Version 1.0 contained no advertising SDK. Version 1.1 adds a small Google AdMob banner in a separate bottom area. Advertising requires an internet connection. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, ad/product interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement/analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No precise-location, camera, microphone, contacts or shared-file access is requested.

We request non-personalised ads. This does not mean that no data is collected: ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention can still require data. We use Google's User Messaging Platform to check privacy requirements at launch and show any required consent form. The advertising SDK is not initialized or asked for ads until that process permits requests. If the consent update or form fails, the course remains usable without ads.

Your advertising choices

Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, “Ad privacy choices” appears in the native bottom area. Use it to revisit your choices. We stop and remove the current banner while the privacy form is open. Resetting course progress does not reset advertising consent. Android's privacy settings can let you reset or delete your advertising ID; available controls depend on your Android version. AdMob and Google apply their own retention and deletion policies. You can contact us about information you have sent directly to support.

See Google's privacy policy and Google advertising information.

External links, Google Play and support

Optional references open in your browser; those websites have their own privacy practices. Google Play separately processes store, purchase and device information under Google's policies. If you email support, we receive your address and the information you send and use it to respond to your enquiry. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Audience and changes

Scientific Roots is intended for adult/general education, not as a child-directed service. If future releases change the data practices, we will update this policy and the store declarations. The current advertising implementation requests Google's G-rated ad-content filter, but advertising content is delivered by Google.

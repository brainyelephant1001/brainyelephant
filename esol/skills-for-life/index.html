Reading · Lesson R1 · Criteria 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 2.3

Reading 1: A message about a class

Learning goal: read a short familiar message and find its main information. This is formative practice only.

Message from the learning centre

Hello Sam,

Your English class is on Monday at 10 am. The class is in Room 4. Please bring a pen and a notebook.

See you soon,
Maria

1. What day is the class?
2. What time does the class begin?
3. Which room is the class in?
4. What should Sam bring?

Practice scores are not qualification results. Completion is kept in this browser only; teacher observation and Gateway-controlled assessment are separate.

Writing · Practice W1 · Criteria 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 2.2

Writing 1: A simple message

Write three short sentences to a teacher. Say your name, the day you attend class, and what you bring. Use a capital letter at the start and a full stop at the end.

Needs teacher review: a saved text is not automatically marked correct; sentence construction, legibility where relevant, spelling, layout and punctuation must be checked by a qualified tutor.

Speaking & Listening · Tutor-led practice SL1 · Criteria 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 3.1, 4.1–4.2

Speaking & Listening 1: At the learning centre

With a tutor or partner: listen to a spoken class time and room number; repeat the information; ask for clarification; introduce yourself; say which subject you like and why.

  1. Listen to a tutor give a one-step instruction and follow it.
  2. Say your name and the class you attend.
  3. Ask: “What time does the class start?”
  4. Take turns in a short conversation about things you like or dislike.

This unit cannot be assessed from multiple-choice questions alone. Audio materials, supervised speaking activities, observation and evidence procedures will be developed with the awarding organisation or an approved centre.

Formal assessment and approval requirements

Gateway's specification calls for Reading: four supervised tasks, approximately one hour; Writing: three supervised tasks, up to 45 minutes; and separate Speaking & Listening assessment arrangements. Brainy Elephant's self-paced online exercises are practice activities and do not replace these assessments.

Gateway centre recognition and specific qualification approval are required before delivering the regulated qualification. We must check staff, quality assurance, identity checks, assessment and access arrangements with Gateway.

Writing W07 — Complete a simple appointment form

Practice using fictional details only. Write a first name, surname, telephone number, appointment day and reason. A tutor must review accuracy and layout.

Writing W08 — Explain an absence

Write to your teacher. Say you cannot attend class today and when you will return. Check capital letters and punctuation.

Hello Ms Brown, I am sorry. I cannot come to class today. I will return on Friday. Thank you, Sam.

Speaking & Listening S07 — Ask for repetition

Tutor says aloud: “Please go to Room 6 and ask for the blue form.” Learner asks for clarification, then repeats the instruction. Tutor observes understanding and request language.

Speaking & Listening S08 — A short discussion about travel

In pairs, ask how your partner travels to class. Respond with a simple preference and take turns. A tutor records observations; website clicks do not prove spoken competence.

ESOL Learning

Independent English-language learning resources, with practical lessons, repeatable practice questions and progress tracking.

Development previews — free to explore. These resources are being reviewed and tested. Level completion and practice results currently stay in your browser on this device. Learner accounts are in testing; final-test eligibility is not yet synchronised between devices, and no payment or certificate issuing facility is active. These are not regulated ESOL qualifications.

Use ESOL on your phone or computer

The same website works on both. On supported Android browsers choose Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu. On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. No app-store download is required for this web version.

No paid access or cross-device progress syncing is active yet. The prototype is currently free to explore.

Learner accounts — development preview

We're connecting individual learner accounts and progress across devices. Email registration and confirmation have been tested, but accounts and cross-device progress are still in development. This is not a public enrolment service.

Request account and data deletion

This page applies to Brainy Elephant student accounts used with our educational website and Android apps, including the Science, Maths, English and ESOL courses.

How to request deletion

  1. Email brainyelephant1001@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Brainy Elephant account, if possible.
  2. Use the subject line Brainy Elephant Account Deletion Request.
  3. State that you want your account and associated personal data deleted. If you cannot email from your registered address, explain this so we can verify your request securely.

Request account deletion by email

You do not need to pay a fee or install an app to submit a deletion request. Never include your password or payment card details in the email.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the student account and personal data controlled by Brainy Elephant that is no longer needed, including associated profile details, saved learning progress, course enrolment and entitlement records, and exam-related data where no retention obligation applies. Deleting an account can remove access to purchased courses and previously issued certificates.

Information that may be retained

Some transaction, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or compliance records may need to be kept where required or permitted by law. Where such records must be retained, we will restrict their use to those purposes and keep them only for the applicable legal retention period. Data in operational backups may remain until those backups expire under the applicable backup retention cycle. We will explain any material exceptions and their retention periods in our response to your request.

Time to respond

We aim to respond to verified requests without undue delay, normally within one month, subject to any extension permitted by applicable data-protection law. If further identity verification or clarification is needed, we will explain what is required.

Other deletion requests

If you only want certain information removed while keeping your account, tell us what data is involved in your email. You can also consult our Privacy Policy.

# Brainy Elephant Master Mark

## Status
**Approved direction — not yet trademark-frozen**

The core Brainy Elephant mark is:

**B — fractal elephant — E**

The elephant emerges through the centre of the initials.

## Core rules
- **B** on the left, **elephant** in the centre, **E** on the right.
- No “BRAINY ELEPHANT” wording in the core mark.
- No slogan in the core mark.
- Primary presentation: electric blue + warm gold on black.
- Once the final vector master is frozen, the elephant geometry stays identical across products.
- WORD MASTER, LIGHT YEARS LEARNING and JOHN SMYTHE RESEARCH are separate product lock-ups and do not alter the core mark.
- “Be Brainy. Be Yourself.” remains provisional and is not part of the core mark.

## Production state
The selected raster concept is the visual reference. Raster exports are suitable for testing but are not a substitute for a true vector master.

Before final production:
1. Reconstruct and freeze the approved design as reproducible vector geometry.
2. Test app-icon, favicon, website-header and print sizes.
3. Produce primary, transparent, monochrome and small-size variants.
4. Perform appropriate trademark clearance before filing or commercial reliance.
5. Freeze the approved geometry and stop regenerating the elephant.

## Brand principle
Brainy Elephant is the parent identity for products intended to help people think, learn, research, create, play and do more for themselves.

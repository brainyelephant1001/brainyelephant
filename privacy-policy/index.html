This policy applies to Brainy Elephant educational applications and services, including English, ESOL, Maths, Science, Word Master, Word Roots, The Great British Test and other products published under Brainy Elephant. It also applies to Brainy Elephant websites where this policy is linked. Features differ between products; the sections below explain when a practice applies.

1. Information you provide

Some products may allow you to provide an email address, account details, course enrolment information, feedback, messages or other information voluntarily. We use this information to provide the requested service, respond to you and administer any applicable account or enrolment. Products that do not offer these features do not collect this information through them.

2. Learning progress and test results

Some apps store answers, scores, achievements and progress locally on your device. Local information may be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Some products, including versions of The Great British Test that enable online statistics, may also submit quiz results such as scores, selected question identifiers, correctness of answers and app version to a database for analysis. Such result submissions are designed not to require your name or email address. As with any online request, technical connection information may be processed by the hosting provider. Different products may use different storage methods.

3. Advertising

Some Brainy Elephant apps, including versions of Word Roots and Word Master, may display advertising through Google AdMob. Where advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic information, subject to the applicable settings and consent requirements. Apps without advertising do not use AdMob merely because other Brainy Elephant products do. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases, subscriptions and payments

Where paid products, purchases or subscriptions are available, transactions may be handled by Google Play, Apple or a payment provider, depending on the product and sales channel. We intend to use Revolut for future direct website payments; this facility is not yet operational. Payment providers process information needed to handle payments under their own policies. Brainy Elephant may receive transaction status, purchase or subscription identifiers, and other information needed to fulfil a purchase or manage support. Payment features are not present in every product.

5. Websites and service providers

Our websites and online features may use hosting, database and infrastructure providers, including services such as Render and Supabase where configured. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, requests and diagnostic logs to deliver, secure and maintain services. Information is shared with service providers only as needed for the relevant feature. We do not sell locally saved learning progress.

6. Purposes and retention

We process information to operate and improve educational products, save progress, provide requested services, administer payments where applicable, respond to enquiries, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations. Locally stored data remains until removed or overwritten according to the app's behaviour. Data held by providers may be retained according to operational needs, applicable law and their policies. Contact us regarding requests concerning information under our control.

7. Children and intended audiences

Brainy Elephant publishes educational products for different audiences. Individual products may have different intended age groups. Where a product is directed at children, its data handling, advertising settings and any required parental measures must be configured for that audience before publication.

8. Your choices and rights

You may clear local app data or uninstall an app. You can contact us to ask about access to, correction or deletion of personal information held by Brainy Elephant, or to raise a privacy concern. Under UK data protection law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of your data, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. Certain anonymously submitted statistics may not be attributable to an individual user.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as products or data practices change. The date above will be updated when material changes are published. The current policy is made available on this page.

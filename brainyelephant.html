What's Inside Your Computer?

Computers gradually collect dust inside their cases, fans and cooling systems.

Heavy dust build-up can restrict airflow, increase fan noise and make it harder for the cooling system to keep components at suitable operating temperatures.

Maintenance can help keep equipment clean and may identify issues before they become bigger problems.

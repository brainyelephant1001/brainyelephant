ESOL Course Terms – Proposed Launch Policy

26 November 2026. New enrolment is currently paused. These proposed terms describe the intended course offering; final checkout terms will be confirmed before sales reopen.

1. Your course

One purchase provides one personal course run covering Entry 1, Entry 2, Entry 3, Level 1 and Level 2, one final practice test and a downloadable results report. The report is not an accredited certificate or recognised qualification.

2. Price

The introductory price is £29.99 for purchases through 6 May 2027. The planned standard price from 7 May 2027 (UK time) is £49.99. This is a one-off payment, not a subscription.

3. Personal access

Access is assigned to your registered account and is personal and non-transferable. Do not share login credentials, resell access or redistribute course materials without permission. Progress is saved between sessions. There is no fixed completion deadline, subject to the inactivity rule below.

Important: three-month inactivity limit

If you do not access your course for three consecutive calendar months, course access will expire. We intend to send reminders after 60 and 80 days of inactivity. Returning to the course before expiry resets the inactivity period. If you experience technical difficulties or need assistance, contact us before expiry where possible. Contact us if you believe access has expired incorrectly.

4. Completion and repeat purchases

After completing the course, including the final practice test, access to course materials ends. You will be able to retrieve your results report without reopening the course. A new purchase is required to repeat the course or regain access after valid inactivity expiry, subject to your statutory rights. Your account need not be deleted.

5. Cancellation, refunds and consumer rights

Nothing in these terms limits your statutory rights. UK consumers generally have a 14-day cancellation period for online contracts, subject to applicable exceptions. For digital content supplied immediately, loss of the cancellation right requires the applicable express consent and acknowledgement before supply begins, together with the required confirmation. Different rules may apply to services. We will confirm the applicable cancellation arrangements at checkout before taking payment. Your rights concerning faulty, misdescribed or undelivered content remain unaffected. Contact us to request cancellation, a refund or help with access.
